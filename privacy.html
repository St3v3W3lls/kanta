Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Cloth Couture ("we", "us") runs the Cloth Couture app, a marketplace where independent clothing shops list their items and customers order from them. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to customers, guests and shop owners.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password (passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our hosting provider; we can never see them).
  • Delivery details: the addresses you save, including the recipient's name and phone number.
  • Orders: what you order, from which shop, the delivery method, any note you add, and the order and payment status the shop records.
  • Shop applications and shop profiles: shop name, description, logo and banner images, phone and Mobile Money numbers, city, pickup address and delivery fees, and the product photos and details a shop lists.
  • Reports: the reason and details you give when you report a listing.
  • Messages to support: anything you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Information created when you use the app:

  • A guest session. If you shop without an account, the app creates an anonymous session on your device so your cart, addresses and orders can be saved. It is not linked to your name unless you enter one.
  • Your cart and wishlist.
  • Settings kept on your device only, such as light or dark mode, notification preferences and whether you have seen the welcome screens.
  • Basic technical data that any internet service receives, such as your IP address, device type and the time of requests, kept in our hosting provider's logs for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics, and we do not collect your location, contacts or payment card details. The app only uses your camera or photo library when you choose a picture to upload (for example a shop logo or product photo).

2. How we use it

  • To run your account or guest session and keep you signed in.
  • To take your orders and pass them to the shops that will deliver them.
  • To let shops manage their listings, orders and customers.
  • To review shop applications and reports, and to keep the marketplace safe, for example by removing counterfeit or misleading listings and preventing fraud.
  • To answer your questions and contact you about your orders or your shop.
  • To improve and fix the app.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We use your information on the basis of providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in running a safe marketplace, your consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations.

3. Who we share it with

  • The shops you order from. When you place an order, that shop receives your name, phone number, delivery address (unless you pick up), the items, and your order note, so it can deliver and collect payment. Shops do not see your email address or your orders with other shops. Each shop is responsible for how it uses your details, and may only use them to fulfil and support your order.
  • Everyone who uses the app can see a shop's public profile (name, description, images, city, phone number, pickup address and delivery fees) and its listings.
  • Our service providers, who process data on our behalf: Supabase (hosting, sign-in, database and file storage) and Google Fonts (the app downloads its typefaces from Google, which receives your IP address). These providers may store data outside Ghana; we use providers that protect it with appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the shops or us.
  • A buyer or successor, if Cloth Couture is ever sold or reorganised, under this same policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. When you delete your account, your profile, saved addresses, cart, wishlist and shop memberships are deleted straight away, and any copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed within 30 days. Order records are kept, no longer linked to your account, for up to 6 years because we and the shops need them for tax, accounting and disputes. Reports you made are kept without your name. Guest sessions that have no orders may be deleted after 90 days without activity. Reports and shop decisions are kept as long as needed to keep the marketplace safe.

5. Your rights and choices

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and similar laws, you can ask to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it (you can edit your profile and addresses in the app at any time);
  • delete your account and personal information;
  • object to or limit how we use it.

To delete your account, open the app and go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete account. If you can't use the app, or for any other request, contact us using the details below from the email or phone number on your account. We will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and our database only lets each person reach their own information: customers see their own orders, and shops see only the orders placed with them. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. You must be at least 18 to open a shop. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will change the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app before they take effect.
