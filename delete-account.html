Delete your Cloth Couture account

Last updated 17 November 2026

You can delete your Cloth Couture account at any time, in the app or by contacting us. This page explains how, and what happens to your information.

In the app

  • Open Cloth Couture and go to Profile → Edit Profile.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

If you own a shop, contact us first so we can close it. If you have orders on the way, you can delete your account once they are delivered or cancelled. Pending orders are cancelled automatically.

Without the app

Contact us from the email address or phone number on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 30 days. Email support@clothcouture.com or call / WhatsApp +233 00 000 0000.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Saved delivery addresses, cart and wishlist

These are deleted immediately; copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

What is kept

  • Records of orders you placed, no longer linked to your account, for up to 6 years, because we and the shops need them for tax, accounting and disputes.
  • Listing reports you made, without your name, to keep the marketplace safe.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail.
